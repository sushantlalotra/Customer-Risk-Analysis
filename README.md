# 🔍 Customer Risk Analysis - Power BI Project

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer risk factors to help businesses assess creditworthiness, detect potential fraud, and minimize financial losses. Using Power BI, the dashboard provides a comprehensive view of customer risk scores, delinquency patterns, and risk mitigation strategies.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
1. Evaluate customer risk profiles based on credit history, transactions, and behavioral patterns.
2. Identify high-risk customers to prevent defaults and fraud.
3. Analyze payment behavior and predict potential delinquencies.
4. Provide actionable insights for financial decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Features
- **Dashboard Highlights**:
  - **Customer Risk Score**: Categorization into high, medium, and low-risk groups.
  - **Default Prediction**: Probability of late payments or loan defaults.
  - **Transaction Analysis**: Unusual spending patterns and flagged transactions.
  - **Customer Segmentation**: Risk segmentation based on demographics and history.
- **Interactive Reports**:
  - Filter customers by risk category, region, and credit utilization.
  - Drill-through analysis for detailed customer profiles.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Power BI**: For data visualization and risk analysis.
- **Excel/MySQL**: Data sources for storing and preprocessing risk-related data.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: For complex measures and risk calculations.
